## Asset Specifications

**File Formats:**
- PNG with transparency (main format)
- SVG for icons (scalable)
- WebP for web optimization

**Sizes:**
- Hero images: 1920x1080px
- Section illustrations: 1200x800px
- Icons: 256x256px or 512x512px
- Mobile screens: 800x1200px

**Style Guidelines:**
- Soft shadows (no harsh blacks)
- Rounded corners (min 8px radius)
- Subtle glow effects
- Clean, minimal aesthetic
- Professional, trustworthy feel
- Family-friendly, warm atmosphere
